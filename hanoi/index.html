Tower of Hanoi

Move all rings from the left spindle to either of the other two spindles. A larger ring cannot be placed on top of a smaller ring.

Mouse / Touch

Click or tap a spindle to select it, then click or tap another spindle to move the top ring.

Keyboard

1 2 3Select left, middle, or right spindle
RReset the current game
H or ?Show this help

Press two different numbers to move a ring (e.g. 1 3). Press the same number twice to cancel.
